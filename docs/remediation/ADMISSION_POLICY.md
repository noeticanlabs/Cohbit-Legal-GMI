# Admission policy

Verified admission requires:

1. an adapter classified `Verified`;
2. independently resolved source text and source identity;
3. an exact quotation link or a versioned human-reviewed evidence link;
4. a configured target forum and non-fabricated jurisdiction metadata;
5. an auditable, non-stale status trace;
6. committed, non-circular rule/fact premises when applying a rule;
7. adverse-authority search provenance and disclosure;
8. valid bounded rational policy values;
9. stability and non-negative resulting valuation.

Generator confidence, witness-supplied support scores, self-declared authority, mock adapters, and local PDF presence cannot authorize verified admission.
