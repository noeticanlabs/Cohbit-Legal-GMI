# CohBit Legal GMI — Commercial-First Licensing Policy

**Version: internal proposal 0.1 | 2026-11-07 | Not an operative license**

## Business objective
The authorized rights holder retains ownership of the first-party implementation and charges for *commercial production use*, whether or not the customer reports a net profit. No revenue or profit threshold automatically exempts a business from commercial licensing.

## Proposed use classification

| Use | Proposed classification | Required authorization |
|---|---|---|
| Reading non-confidential public technical papers | Publication-specific terms | No implied software license |
| Internal development, personal research, academic experiments | Evaluation only | Signed, scope-limited evaluation license |
| Independent mathematical or security audit | Evaluation/audit | Written audit scope, confidentiality terms if appropriate |
| Commercial-company non-production testing | Evaluation only | Signed evaluation license; no operational business reliance |
| Paid integration or custom research pilot | Commercial services + evaluation | Signed statement of work and applicable license |
| Internal production at a commercial business | Commercial | Paid business license |
| Billable professional workflows (including law firms) | Commercial | Paid professional-services license |
| SaaS/API/hosted service or sale of verification outputs | Commercial | Paid hosted-services or OEM license |
| Embedding, redistribution, white-label or resale | Commercial | Paid OEM/distribution license |
| Government, nonprofits or pro bono work | Case-specific | Written license; discretionary waiver only if explicitly granted |

**Production use** includes use of outputs in a real operational or client decision, even without hosting, resale, positive profit, or fees for a particular transaction. Synthetic testing without such operational reliance is ordinarily evaluation.

## Proposed payment structure
- Research/evaluation: no software license fee **only when separately authorized**, ordinarily up to 60 days; paid support or pilots can be billed separately.
- Commercial internal use: fixed recurring subscription per agreed deployment, user count, tenant, or volume.
- Professional services: subscription and agreed use limits; no license grants authority to practice law.
- Hosted/embedded/OEM: negotiated annual minimum plus metered fees or a percentage of precisely defined **attributable gross receipts** if the parties choose revenue sharing.
- Enterprise/public sector: negotiated annual, security, integration, SLA, and support schedules.

No price, percentage, minimum, discounted rate, free allowance, exclusivity, or revenue share is effective without a signed order form. There is no automatic equity, partnership, agency, joint venture, or ownership transfer.

## Product restrictions
Free evaluation does not authorize real-case production reliance, customer-facing hosted services, redistribution, commercial integration, or filings. Commercial rights must identify the precise program/version, permitted functionality, users, environments, use cases, territory, sublicensing scope (if any), and security duties.

## Protection and dissemination
- Keep the core implementation private or distribute it only under controlled agreements.
- Publish research results and proof descriptions separately if desirable; publication of mathematics does not itself grant software rights.
- Allow audited source access without implying a general license, but respect applicable law and the separate rights of dependency authors.
- Limit acceptance of external code contributions pending a contributor-rights policy.
- Consider whether a small independent checker might later be separately licensed; **no such open-source grant is made now**.

## Commercial-readiness boundary
CohBit is still a research prototype. Signed commercial evaluation and development services are possible, but representation as dependable legal-advice software, guaranteed deadline computation, or production-qualified legal verification is **not authorized by this licensing policy**. Maintain separate independent quality and legal-domain validation gates.

## Change control
The copyright owner/authorized licensor and counsel must approve any operative change. Only fully executed agreements grant customer rights. Repository policy edits do not retroactively amend earlier customer contracts.
