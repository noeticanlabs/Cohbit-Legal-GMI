## Non-negotiable distinction
Commercial licensing terms do not establish legal or mathematical reliability. The engine is not qualified for live legal determinations or automated filing. Any live-use contract must set a permitted scope, professional oversight, data controls, and liability allocations after independent validation.

## Adoption workflow
1. Confirm the legal rights owner, assignments, and signatory authority.
2. Conduct third-party and distribution-rights review; obtain lawyer review of both agreement templates.
3. Approve pricing and product/usage order schedules.
4. Provide specific terms to each prospect and obtain signatures **before distributing evaluation builds or granting production access**.
5. Record customer identity, signed form/version, dates, installations, permitted use, renewals, and invoices. Never treat a GitHub invite as an executed software license.
