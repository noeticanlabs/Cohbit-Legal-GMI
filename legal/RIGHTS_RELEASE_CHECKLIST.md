**Blocking checklist. Do not issue production licenses or market legal reliability until required sign-offs are complete.**

## A. Legal ownership and licensor identity — BLOCKING

- [ ] Obtain appropriate signed assignment or written license authorization where rights move between parties; do not rely on changing a README.
- [ ] Review employment/contractor/work-made-for-hire and any third-party/AI-generated contribution rights.
- [ ] Confirm signer authority and establish a genuine company licensing contact channel.

## B. Third-party distribution rights — BLOCKING for releases
- [ ] Generate and review a version-specific Rust and Lean SBOM.
- [ ] Preserve mandatory license texts, attribution, patent, and other notices.
- [ ] Inspect the archived Cohbit-Legal-GMI-1.0.0-hardened.zip and all release artifacts for contrary licensing language.
- [ ] Confirm no incorporated code is owned by outside contributors without appropriate grant.

## C. Licensing instruments — BLOCKING for external customer use
- [ ] Obtain qualified intellectual-property and software-contract counsel review of evaluation and commercial terms.
- [ ] Resolve governing law, venue, legal notices, liability, indemnities, confidentiality, nonwaivable rights, export and tax obligations.
- [ ] Finalize signed Schedule A and any DPA, SOW, SLA, or OEM/royalty addendum.
- [ ] Set an authorized evaluation/distribution process; do not treat GitHub access as executed contract acceptance.
- [ ] Set price lists, account qualification, billing, renewal and collection procedures.
- [ ] Establish signed agreement storage and license inventory with customer, version, permitted use, and dates.

## D. Product qualification — BLOCKING for live legal use
- [ ] Complete independently assessed legal domain qualification, held-out testing and applicable professional review.
- [ ] Resolve certificate-to-civic text/receipt binding and remaining proof integrity issues.
- [ ] Implement production privacy, security, retention, and consent controls.
- [ ] Approve only precisely scoped claims about formal soundness and legal applicability.

## E. Publishing and safety checks
- [ ] Keep repository private unless specifically authorized; source-access scope and NDA separately documented.
- [ ] Review releases, package registries, CI artifacts, and website claims for accidental publication/grants.
- [ ] Choose whether any independent checker will ever use a different license; do not imply automatic open sourcing.
- [ ] Get final rights-holder and counsel signoff **before merging externally operative licensing terms or sending customer contracts**.
