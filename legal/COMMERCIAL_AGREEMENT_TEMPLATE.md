## 1. Licensed Software; license scope

## 2. Commercial-use definition and trigger
“Commercial Production Use” includes using the Software or its outputs as part of a business's real operations, client work, revenue-generating services, paid products, or hosted offerings, **regardless of whether the business has positive net income**. Free research/testing exceptions do not apply by default; each must be granted by a distinct written agreement.

## 3. Fees, payment, and usage reports
The agreed subscription, deployment, support, transaction, minimum commitment and/or royalty fees are solely those stated in Schedule A. Set invoicing frequency, currency, tax responsibility, due date, late fees (where lawful), metering, overage rates, and termination/refund treatment in Schedule A and counsel-reviewed terms. No automatic percentage of a customer's revenue is imposed by this template.

If usage reporting is required, define measurable events and privacy-preserving reporting methods. Audit rights, notice and frequency, and who bears audit costs must be reasonable and separately specified.

## 4. Optional revenue share (requires executed addendum)
If the parties agree to revenue share, the signed addendum must define the **covered product**, calculation base (such as attributable gross receipts), allowed refunds/taxes/deductions, treatment of bundled products, reporting and payment frequency, reasonable record-retention period, confidentiality, audit mechanism, minimum payments, and post-termination settlement. Revenue sharing **does not by itself create an equity partnership** or joint venture.

No royalty obligation exists without signed, completed terms.

## 5. Ownership, improvements, and third-party components
Licensor retains the rights it owns in the Software; no sale, assignment, or patent license is implied except to the extent expressly provided. Customer retains rights in its data and independently owned intellectual property. Treatment of custom development, deliverables, improvements and contributions is specified in a separately signed SOW. Licenses to third-party components remain governed by the third parties' own terms.

## 6. Data security, confidentiality, and professional oversight
Customer is responsible for lawfully obtaining data and for appropriate human review of consequential decisions. Required privacy, breach reporting, tenant isolation, retention, deletion, encryption and data-processing obligations must be defined by the applicable DPA/security exhibit. Neither party may assume that the current research prototype is suitable for live legal decisions.

No party may represent CohBit as guaranteeing legal accuracy or replacing professional legal judgment. Production deployment for legal-advice or legal-deadline reliance remains barred until a separate, approved scope and independently validated qualification are in place.

## 7. Restrictions
Except as permitted in a signed order or by nonwaivable law, Customer may not redistribute proprietary code, remove ownership notices, operate unauthorized hosted or embedded offerings, exceed licensed user/site/deployment/volume limits, or provide the Software to unrelated third parties. Reverse-engineering restrictions must be drafted consistently with applicable mandatory legal rights.

## 8. Term; suspension; termination; transition
The term, renewal, nonpayment suspension, material-breach notice/cure, termination assistance, post-termination cessation, archival obligations, deletion/return of confidential material and survival provisions must be defined in Schedule A/B and approved by counsel. Avoid relying on repository-file changes to revise signed customer contracts.

## 9. Warranties, disclaimers, allocation of risk
Warranty scope, permitted remedies, support commitments, express limitations, liability caps, exclusions, indemnity allocation, insurance (if required), and regulatory/professional representations are **open issues for qualified legal review**. The customer must not be promised a guarantee of legal results or that existing code is production-ready.

## 10. General legal terms
Complete governing law, courts/venue or arbitration, notices, assignment, change control, export/sanctions compliance, third-party beneficiary provisions, signature/counterpart rules, and entire agreement in counsel-approved final terms.
