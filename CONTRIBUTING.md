# Contributing to CohBit Legal GMI

CohBit Legal GMI is planned as **AGPL-3.0-only** open-source software. We welcome independent scrutiny, reproducibility reports, and bug reports.

**Until a contributor license / dual-licensing permissions policy is finalized, please do not submit unsolicited code patches, pull requests containing code from outside sources, or third-party materials.** Open an issue to discuss a proposed contribution first.

Why this matters: The AGPL grants strong freedoms to recipients, but it does not automatically transfer ownership of a contributor's work or authorize the original project owner to sell that contribution under separate proprietary terms.

Research publications and issues must not include confidential customer information, unapproved proprietary work, or personal legal case details. The project is a research prototype, not legal advice.
