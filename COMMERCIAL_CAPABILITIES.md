# CohBit Legal GMI — Capability Boundary

## Implemented research capabilities

- verifier-gated claim admission with explicit escalation;
- canonical receipts and replay-oriented traces;
- crash-recoverable local persistence;
- source-containing portable bundles;
- external-key Ed25519 bundle authentication;
- deterministic rational arithmetic;
- Lean content-mutation and stability properties.

## Not production-qualified

The project has no included authoritative legal citator, live-law coverage guarantee, externally validated semantic accuracy result, SLA, or completed multi-tenant privacy/security assessment. Local and mock adapters must not be presented as Shepardizing, KeyCiting, or equivalent live status services.

Potential applications—after independent qualification—include evidence-governance experiments, reproducible legal research, and human-controlled civic-information demonstrations. Mission-critical filings, automated legal advice, and autonomous advocacy are outside the current qualification boundary.

No performance, accuracy, certification, or readiness claim should be made without a reproducible artifact identifying the exact code, dataset, environment, and evaluator.
